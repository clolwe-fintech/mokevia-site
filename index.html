Security and control

Your data. Our priority.

Money software has to be careful and hard to break into. This is how we run it.

  • Each institution's data is separated at the database level
  • Role-based access and a full audit trail of sensitive actions
  • Sign-in protected by a bot check and automatic account lock-out, with alerts to our team
  • Data encrypted in transit, with nightly encrypted backups held at two separate providers
  • Restores tested regularly, not just assumed to work
  • Hosted in the UK and EU today, with a Kenyan hosting region planned
